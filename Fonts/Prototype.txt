Windows TrueType font, free for personal use.
Homepage: http://members.aol.com/disneyfonts/pt.html

Please include this Prototype.txt file with any redistribution.

PROTOTYPE is an original font based on the signage typeface used at Walt
Disney World's Epcot theme park. It can also be seen on the monorails and in
Disneyland's Tomorrowland.

The font was created by Justin Callaghan. Comments, questions, complaints,
and love notes were directed by the original notice to jcmagic@yahoo.com.
